JellySling

Stretch the jelly and fling it up! A one-finger squishy climber.

Get it on Google PlayApp page

This is the web version of the Android app. Tap START, then drag back and let go to fling the jelly upward. Keep climbing and beat your best height.

About the game

Stretch the squishy jelly with your finger and let go: it springs upward. JellySling is a one-finger climber all about stretch, fling and cling. Aim with direction and power, grab the next ledge and climb higher.

Features

  • Aim with direction and power
  • Squishy, wobbly stretch physics
  • Grab ledges and keep climbing
  • Rising tension, beat your best height
  • One-finger controls
