Starter Gun: Reaction Survival

Lift your finger the moment the gunshot and flash fire. How many stages can you clear with three lives?

Get it on Google PlayApp page

This is the web version of the Android app. Press and hold the pad at the bottom, then release the instant you hear the gunshot and see the red flash. Turn your sound on for the best experience.

About the game

Hold the blue pad and you will hear "On your marks" and "Set"; after a random wait the gunshot fires with a red flash. The rule is simple: release as fast as you can. But each stage shortens the time limit and mixes in fake sounds and fake flashes to trick you. Chase your best stage and best reaction time before your three lives run out.

Features

  • Reaction time measured to 0.001 s
  • A tighter time limit every stage
  • Fake beeps and fake flashes to fool you
  • Three lives, one record run
  • Best stage and best reaction time saved
