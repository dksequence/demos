About the app

Tap two tiles that show the same picture. They clear if the path between them bends no more than twice. The path runs through empty cells, and may go one step outside the board.

After every match the remaining tiles shift: down, sideways, to the center, or apart. Nine different flow directions mean the same layout never plays the same way. Run out of moves and the board reshuffles for you.

Features

  • Mahjong connect rules: link two matching tiles
  • Nine flow directions that move the board after every match
  • 60 stages, each with three stars and a time limit
  • Three tools: hint, shuffle and bomb
  • 12-language support
