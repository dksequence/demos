Radio Waves

Turn a vintage dial and tune in to radio stations from around the world, streamed live over the internet.

Get it on Google PlayApp page

This is the web version of the Android app. Tap "Next" on the first screen to begin. Stations stream live over the internet and use data. Because of browser security rules, some stations are left out of the web version’s list (the app can reach more stations).

About the app

Radio Waves is a radio app where you find stations from around the world by turning a vintage dial. Browse by country or genre, hop between channels with next, previous and random, and keep the ones you like in your favorites. The station list and streams are provided by a third party (the public Radio Browser directory), and the programming belongs to each station.

What it does

  • Tune stations by turning a vintage dial
  • Browse by country or genre, or search by name
  • Next, previous and random channel hopping
  • Favorites and recently played stations
  • 5 radio skins (vacuum-tube console, field radio, world band, pocket transistor, ham transceiver)
  • Sleep timer
