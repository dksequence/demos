DK Music Box

A retro cassette music player. Healing-music albums play back to back on a cassette-deck screen.

Get it on Google PlayApp page

This is the web version of the Android app. Tap OK in the dialog to start playback. Music streams from YouTube, so an internet connection is required and data usage applies.

About the app

Play DKsequence's healing-music albums continuously on a nostalgic cassette-deck screen. Each long video holds multiple songs (chapters) you can skip between one by one, with shuffle and repeat support. Sit back and enjoy the music on a warm, brushed-metal player.

The music and videos played in this app are created using AI technology.

Features

  • Continuous album playback
  • Jump between chapters (songs) within a video
  • Shuffle and repeat modes
  • Cassette-deck styled player screen
  • 12-language support
