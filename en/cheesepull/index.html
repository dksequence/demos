Cheese Pull

Pull the cheese slowly and stretch it as far as you dare before it snaps. How far will it go?

Get it on Google PlayApp page

This is the web version of the Android app. Tap Play, press and slowly drag up to stretch the cheese, and let go when it gets risky. Vibration may not be supported in the web version.

About the app

Cheese Pull is a stretching game about melted pizza cheese. Pull too fast and it snaps; let it go cold and it snaps. When it turns red it is about to break, so you decide whether to bank it or push on, and letting go splices it for an even longer pull. Climb past a chair, a person, a car, a house and a hot-air balloon, up to the clouds and space, and chase your longest record.

What it does

  • Orders mode with stages to clear
  • Endless Stretch and a Daily Pull for record chasing
  • Bank it or push on, right before the snap
  • 5 shops, from a street oven to a master kitchen
  • Collect cheeses and share a record card
  • 12-language support
